E-Verify

Version 1.0

E-Verify Extension is a real-time FDA cosmetic products verification for major e-marketplace platforms (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Facebook Marketplace) in the Philippines.

How it Works

3 simple steps:

Highlight

Shop for cosmetic products on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, or Facebook Marketplace, then highlight an item to check its verification status.

Scan

E-Verify will then detect & check the cosmetic product from the FDA database

Verify

See if detected cosmetic product is FDA Registered, Unregistered, and Suspicious (needs further checking).
